Procurement

Three agreements. One RFQ path.

Supply agreements cover price, quantity, delivery and responsibility for defects. Service agreements define scope, quality and response time.

  1. 01
    Received by CRM

    Your focal point takes the request.

  2. 02
    Entered in the system

    The specification is recorded before anyone prices it.

  3. 03
    Stock, or source it

    A stocked item is offered from the warehouse. Anything else is sourced from three or more suppliers.

  4. 04
    Best quote selected

    Price, lead time and fitness for the specification.

  5. 05
    Quotation sent

    You have one document to approve or mark up.

01

Fixed quantity

A fixed quantity of goods or services inside a set timeframe. The right shape when demand is predictable.

02

Reimbursable

Daily reimbursement against actual usage or work. Common where the cost moves from one day to the next.

03

Time and material

Hours worked plus the materials used. Flexible when the project itself is still moving.
